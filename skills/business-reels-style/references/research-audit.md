# Research audit: six creator references

**Purpose:** preserve the evidence boundary behind `../SKILL.md`. This is not an exhaustive ranking of all Reels. It records what was accessible during the research pass and what the evidence does not establish.

**Important limitation:** the Instagram web pages could not be opened and played consistently in the research environment. Third-party mirrors and analytics tools were used as discovery aids. A profile tile, still image, transcript, public cross-post, or aggregate analytics page is not equivalent to viewing the exact Reel start to finish. For this reason, no claim is made that the actual top-trending Reel for each account was fully inspected.

## Evidence glossary

- **Exact Reel inspected:** the exact Reel's moving image was played and observed.
- **Still/cross-post inspected:** at least one associated still frame or cross-post image was visible, but the complete Instagram Reel's motion was not validated.
- **Transcript/post text:** a caption or transcript could be read; this does not reveal exact visual or sound design.
- **Aggregate analytics:** follower or average-view figures were visible; these cannot identify a particular top Reel.
- **Metric ambiguous:** a mirror displayed numbers, but the returned page did not label them clearly enough to assert that they were view counts.

## Recommended follow-up audit to achieve the user's strict requirement

To produce an honestly source-verified “top videos from every profile” edition:

1. For each account, gather a defined candidate set of Reels and capture the exact post URL, posted date, view/play count, likes/comments if public, and capture date.
2. State ranking scope and sort only by verified views/plays (or a clearly disclosed alternative when views are unavailable).
3. Play the selected Reels and save short time-coded observations or screenshots for the opening, 2–3 representative middle beats, key reveal, and ending.
4. Record actual frame dimensions, measured text location, sampled colors, typeface candidates, average shot duration, transition types, music/SFX behavior, and subtitle style. Mark exact values as approximations where video compression or font identification prevents certainty.
5. Update the account-specific sections in `SKILL.md` only after this evidence exists. Keep this audit with the skill so future edits can distinguish new direct evidence from older assumptions.
