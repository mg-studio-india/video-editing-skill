# plan.md template

Keep it short enough to read in two minutes. The user approves this, not the JSON.

```markdown
# <Project title>: plan v<N>

**Program:** long-form (12 min talk, 4 sections) · **Aspect:** 16:9, plus 2 Shorts · **Audio:** youtube (-14 LUFS)
**Reference:** <reference video, if any, and the 2 or 3 numbers we are matching>

## Treatments
- **Layered-head**: the three big numbers in the hook, said on camera to a plain wall (clean matte).
- **B-roll**: every tool named gets its real window; two generated shots of you (below).
- **Whiteout**: once, into section 3 ("and then it broke").

## Hook (first 40 s)
Pattern: result first + open loop. Promise on screen by 1.2 s: "Edit a video in 10 minutes".
Loop: "Which of the three tools matters most?" planted at "three tools", paid off at "the one that matters" (section 4).

## Beats
| # | When you say... | On screen | Treatment | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "in ten minutes" | line: **Edit a video in 10 minutes** | caption line | the promise, readable muted |
| 2 | "three tools" | HyperFrames · whisper.cpp · Veo, one per word | card-list | the structure of the video |
| 3 | "every morning" | gen-01: you at your desk at dawn, 3 s | B-roll | the scene, not proof |
| ... | | | | |

On-screen moments: 17 (2-min talk, data-rich: 10 s × 0.7 = 7 s each).

## Inserts
| Id | Kind | What | Source | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mg-01 | motion graphic | 3-step pipeline | HyperFrames component | free |
| own-01 | your footage | screen rec 01:12-01:20 | raw/screen.mov | free |
| gen-01 | generated video | you at your desk at dawn, slow push-in | Veo (gate 3) | ~$<estimate> |

## Shorts (if any)
1. "The real reason editing takes so long" (0:42-1:31, ~49 s) - hook line + payoff, no context needed.

## Concerns
- The section 2 explanation runs 70 s with nothing to show except the face: I propose a diagram (mg-02); approve?
- "whisper" is said twice in the hook; the list item anchors to the second one.
```
