# Recording so the cut works

Share this with the user before a shoot. Every point saves a manual fix later.

- **4K at 30 fps** (29.97 is fine), standard colour, flat: no in-camera zooms, captions or transitions. 1080p goes soft
  past ~112% zoom, and a 9:16 Short cropped from 1080p is only 606 px wide before scaling; 4K holds 200% and gives
  Shorts a sharp 1215 px crop.
- **The mic on every file**, one framing per session, a second of silence at each end (the cleanup measures the room
  from it).
- **Fluff a line? Pause about two seconds and restart the whole sentence from its first word.** The last take wins.
  Redo the full sentence when a number, name or price came out wrong. "Cut that" or "scratch that" drops a stretch.
- **No spoken slates or notes to yourself** (they are speech and get kept); clap or hold fingers up instead.
- **Say lists as lists, name tools out loud** (logos come from the name), say money in full ("a thousand dollars a
  month", never "1K"), point where a graphic should go, leave the pause after a joke, end a segment on its outro line.
- **For Shorts:** keep your head and hands inside the middle third of the frame for the lines you want as Shorts; a
  9:16 crop keeps only that third.
- **For split-screen or layered-head:** a plain background behind you makes the subject matte cleaner.
- **Two-file setups** (face camera + screen recording): record both at once, both with the mic, one clap at the start,
  the same file name stem.
